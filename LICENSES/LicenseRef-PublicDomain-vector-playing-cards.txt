Public domain (vector-playing-cards)

The playing-card images in assets/cards/ are the work of Byron Knoll, from the vector-playing-cards
project. The centre pip of ace_of_spades.png is a rescaled copy of a pip from 2_of_spades.png, also
Byron Knoll's work: it replaced an ornate centre spade by another artist, which carries no public
domain statement and has been removed. See assets/cards/ATTRIBUTION.md.

Source: https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/ (Google Code Archive)

Byron Knoll's statement, as given on that project:

    "These images are released into the public domain - attribution is appreciated but not required."

whole licence text. Attribution is appreciated and is given in assets/cards/ATTRIBUTION.md.
